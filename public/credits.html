Credits & attribution.

FlashHub hosts almost nothing of its own. Nearly every game here was preserved, ported or emulated by someone else, and this page exists to say who.

Game preservation & porting

genizy/web-port
Unity and Godot games ported to run in the browser. FlashHub streams these on demand rather than redistributing them.
slqntdevss/quasar
The bulk of the catalogue — Flash, HTML5 and WebGL titles plus the emulator shells. Served through this hub's proxy from the project's own CDN.
aukak/gameports
An additional library of browser game ports, used when a local copy has been synced.
Internet Archive
The Software Library: Flash collection. Individual .swf files are streamed from the Archive; the games remain the work of their original authors.

Runtimes

Ruffle
The Flash Player emulator, in Rust and WebAssembly, that makes every Flash title here playable without a plugin. FlashHub would not exist without it.
EmulatorJS
The console emulator shells you can load your own ROMs into.

Infrastructure

jsDelivr
Open-source CDN that serves most game assets.
Steam Web API
Optional profile, library and achievement data for players who link a Steam account. Steam and the Steam logo are trademarks of Valve Corporation. FlashHub is not affiliated with or endorsed by Valve.
DiceBear
The generated profile avatars.

Design & code

Archivo
Typeface by Omnibus-Type, under the SIL Open Font License.
Lucide
The interface icons, under the ISC licence.
Open-source stack
React, Vite, Express, Tailwind CSS, Motion, and TypeScript.

Copyright & takedowns

The games are the property of their original authors and studios. FlashHub is a preservation front end: it does not claim ownership of any title, and it proxies or links to files hosted elsewhere rather than republishing them as its own.

If you hold the rights to something here and would like it removed, get in touch through github.com/rustybusgaming and it will be taken down.

FlashHub is a personal, non-commercial project by rustybusgaming, run from New Zealand.
