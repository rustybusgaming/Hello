Privacy policy.

Last updated 20 August 2026

Who runs this

FlashHub is operated by rustybusgaming. It is a personal, non-commercial project run from New Zealand. Questions about this policy or your data go through github.com/rustybusgaming.

What is collected

You can browse and play the entire catalogue without an account. Nothing below is collected until you choose to create one.

Account
Username, email address, and a password. The password is never stored — only a PBKDF2-SHA512 hash with a per-account random salt. Optionally a display name, a short bio and an avatar choice.
Session
A random session token in an httpOnly, SameSite=Strict cookie, valid for 30 days. It is not readable by page scripts and is not used for tracking or advertising.
Play data
Cumulative playtime in minutes, achievements earned, and any save slots you create. A save slot stores the game's own progress data, plus the slot name, notes and score you type.
Steam link (optional)
If you connect Steam, your Steam ID, persona name, avatar, profile URL, level and game count are stored. Library and achievement data is fetched live and not retained. You can unlink at any time, which deletes all of it.
In your browser only
Favourites, recently played, and per-game progress are kept in your browser's local storage. They never reach the server unless you are signed in and save a slot.

There are no analytics, no advertising, no tracking pixels and no third-party cookies set by FlashHub itself. IP addresses are held briefly in memory to rate-limit sign-in attempts, and are not written to disk or linked to your account.

Third parties

Games are streamed from other people's servers, so playing one causes requests to them. Your browser or this server contacts:

  • archive.org, cdn.jsdelivr.net and raw.githubusercontent.com — game files. These are proxied through this server, so those hosts see the server's address rather than yours.
  • unpkg.com — the Ruffle Flash runtime, loaded directly by your browser.
  • fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com — the typeface, loaded directly by your browser.
  • api.dicebear.com — generated avatars, loaded directly by your browser.
  • api.steampowered.com and steamcommunity.com — only if you link a Steam account.
Games are third-party code. A game may contain its own analytics or advertising from whenever it was made. FlashHub runs every game in a sandboxed frame on a separate origin so it cannot read your account or session, but it cannot control what a game requests from its own authors' servers.

Where it is kept

Account, session and save data are stored as files on the server this hub runs on. Data is kept for as long as your account exists, and is deleted with it.

If that server sits outside New Zealand, your information is held overseas and protected by that provider's arrangements rather than by New Zealand law directly — principle 12 of the Privacy Act 2020 covers this.

Your choices

  • Export — every save slot can be downloaded as JSON from the player.
  • Correct — display name, bio and avatar are editable at any time.
  • Delete — deleting your account from Account → Security removes the account and all of its save slots immediately and permanently. Browser-local data is cleared by clearing site data.

Under the Privacy Act 2020 you have the right to ask what personal information is held about you and to ask for it to be corrected. For anything you cannot do from the account screen, get in touch through github.com/rustybusgaming. If you are not happy with the response, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner.

Children

FlashHub is not directed at children under 13, and accounts are not knowingly created for them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, get in touch and it will be removed.
